## Natural Voice TTS 1.3.0: pronunciation list and spoken emphasis

**Download:** `NaturalVoiceTTS_Setup_1.3.0.exe` below (~1.8 GB). Uninstall 1.2.0 first, then run the installer.

### What's new
- **Pronunciation list.** Names and brands the voice gets wrong can be given exact pronunciations — for example "Cowork" said as "Co work". The app ships a built-in list; your own entries go in `%APPDATA%\NaturalVoiceTTS\pronunciations.json` and win over the built-in ones. A word matches in any capitalization (unless the entry has a capital inside it, like "iOS"), `'s` is handled for you, and edits apply from the next message with no restart.
- **Spoken emphasis.** Words in **bold** or *italics*, and ALL-CAPS words that are shouted rather than acronyms ("NOT", "NEVER", but not "API" or "GPU"), are said a little slower, higher and louder. On by default.

What you see on screen is never changed; only the spoken copy is.

### Testing
- Both features were checked by ear in A/B listening and approved.
- 347 automated tests pass, including a check that no word is lost between the text and the speech.
- Emphasis adds only a small first-read cost (worst case about +6% on the first emphasized chunk) and nothing after.
- Same Kokoro model, default voice (af_heart) and speed as 1.2.0.
