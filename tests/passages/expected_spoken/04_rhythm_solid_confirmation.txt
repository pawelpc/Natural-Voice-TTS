The two initial failures are worth pinning down, though, because the signature matters.
The dropped-sentence bug we fixed was silent partial loss — you'd hear most of a response with a few sentences missing.
Two attempts failing outright and then working is a different shape, and almost certainly a startup/timing issue, not a regression.
The most likely causes, in order:
One. App still warming up.
After install/reboot, the tray app takes about 6 to 9 seconds to load the Kokoro model (your log showed seventeen oh six twenty seven to seventeen oh six thirty six).
Any speak before it finishes returns Error: Named pipe … not found — because the pipe listener starts only after the engine is ready.
Two. Pipe reconnect race.
The listener holds a single pipe instance.
Between one client disconnecting and the loop recreating the pipe, there's a millisecond gap where a fast follow-up speak can get "pipe not found" / "all instances busy."
Two rapid early calls could hit this.
